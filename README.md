# Hi, I'm Willmers Hernández 👋

I'm an **Electromechanical and Mechanical Engineer** pursuing a **master's degree in Pure Mathematics at UASD**, in the Dominican Republic. I work in engineering and commissioning for LNG facilities, and use programming to explore mathematics and solve practical problems.

My background in **graphic design** supports my front-end work through visual communication, typography, layout, and interface design.

## My focus

**Mathematical analysis, numerical methods, and scientific computing**: implementing algorithms, studying convergence, and connecting theory with computational experiments. I'm also getting into **machine learning and optimization algorithms**, particularly quasi-Newton methods and their applications to model training.
