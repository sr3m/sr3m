# Hi, I'm Willmers Hernández 👋

I'm an **Electromechanical and Mechanical Engineer** pursuing a **master's degree in Pure Mathematics at UASD**, in the Dominican Republic. I work in engineering and commissioning for LNG facilities, and use programming to explore mathematics and solve practical problems.

## My focus

My studies center on **mathematical analysis, numerical methods, and scientific computing**. I enjoy implementing algorithms, studying convergence, and connecting mathematical theory with computational experiments.

I'm also getting into **machine learning and optimization algorithms**, with a particular interest in quasi-Newton methods and how numerical optimization can support model training.
